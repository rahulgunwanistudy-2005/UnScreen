# Consent: UnScreen test session

**What this is.** A student project for the Taskforge hackathon (October 2026). UnScreen tries to make a shared screen in a video meeting readable with a screen reader. We want to learn whether it helps you, and where it doesn't.

**What you'll do.** About 30 minutes online. We share a test spreadsheet made up for this session, which contains no real data. You try three tasks: find a value, say what changed after an edit, and say what the presenter's pointer is on. You do them once with NVDA's built-in OCR and once with UnScreen. Then we ask a few questions.

**It's voluntary.** You can skip any task or question, take a break, or stop at any time, without giving a reason. You can also ask us to delete your results afterwards.

**What we record.** By default, only written notes: whether each task succeeded, how long it took, and a 1–5 rating of how much you trusted the answer.
- We do **not** record video, your face or your voice unless you tick the box below.
- We publish results without your name.
- We'll only quote you if you agree to the specific quote.

**What leaves your computer.** During the UnScreen part, pictures of the *test* spreadsheet are sent to Google's Gemini API to be read. None of your own screen is shared.
